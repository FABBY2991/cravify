# Glossary

- **Slide** — one full-screen hero panel, owned by a category: colour, headline, line above, cut-out or clip, pieces, featured item, credit.
- **Headline** — the oversized single word behind the food (BURGERLICIOUS).
- **Kicker / line above** — the small spaced-out line above the headline.
- **Cut-out** — the food image with a transparent background that sits in front of the headline.
- **Piece** — a small floating ingredient cut-out (up to four per slide).
- **Clip** — an optional looping video a slide plays instead of its cut-out.
- **Wipe** — the slide change: the next slide's colour sweeps in from the side.
- **Featured item** — the menu item on a slide's product card.
- **Sold out** — shown on the menu, greyed, and refused at checkout. **Hidden** (disabled) — not shown at all.
- **Fulfilment** — pickup or delivery.
- **Tracking link** — `/order/<token>`; the token is the order's secret.
- **Order code** — the short human reference, e.g. CR-4821.
- **Sample order** — seeded by the system for the demo; **visitor order** — placed through checkout.
- **Demo sales** — the day's total of non-cancelled orders; not real money.
- **Today strip** — the admin's orders/sales/active/cancelled counters for the Nairobi day.
- **Reset demo** — deletes every order and lays out fresh samples; the menu is untouched.
