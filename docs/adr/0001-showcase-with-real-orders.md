# 1. A showcase that takes real orders, without payment

Cravify is a portfolio piece, not a restaurant. Its job is to impress in ten
seconds and then hold up when a client clicks around — so "ORDER NOW" starts a
real flow: cart, guest checkout, a saved order, an admin board that updates
live, and a tracking page that follows the order. There is no payment step;
M-Pesa (sandbox) can be added later as its own decision.

Because orders are real but never fulfilled, the site says so plainly — a demo
strip on every page, at checkout and on the tracking page — and asks for no
real contact details (see ADR 3).
